- **DeepLearning.AI** — 7 月上线两门 short course：①[Voice for AI Agents and Applications](https://learn.deeplearning.ai/) — 教 embedded voice / voice-layered-on-agents / voice-as-tool 三种集成模式；② [AI Code Review（与 Qodo 合作）](https://community.deeplearning.ai/t/new-course-enroll-in-ai-code-review/894220) — 从 early review 到给 reviewer 上下文，教学员搭 review agent；两门均免费
