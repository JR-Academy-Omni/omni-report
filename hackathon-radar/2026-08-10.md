来源：[LaunchVic AI & DeepTech 资助公告](https://launchvic.org/funding-news/launchvic-announces-new-grant-round-for-ai-and-deeptech-startup-support/) · [Victoria backs next wave of AI founders](https://launchvic.org/announcements/victoria-backs-next-wave-of-ai-and-deeptech-founders/)
